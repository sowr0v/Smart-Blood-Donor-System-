About the platform

Making blood support easier to reach.

Smart Blood Donor System is a community platform intended to help blood donors, patients, hospitals, and blood banks find one another more easily.

Our purpose

Finding a compatible donor can be time-sensitive. The platform is designed to make requests and donor availability easier to discover, while encouraging people to connect directly and confirm details with the relevant medical facility.

Use with care

Information on this site is not medical advice and donor availability is not guaranteed. Confirm blood group, eligibility, timing, and arrangements with qualified healthcare professionals. In a life-threatening emergency, contact emergency services.
