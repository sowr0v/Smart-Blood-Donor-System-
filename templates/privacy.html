Data Security

Privacy Policy

Your data is safe with us.

1. Information Collection

We collect information necessary to operate the blood donation platform. This includes your name, contact information (phone number), blood group, and location data when you interact with the service.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely for the purpose of connecting blood donors with those in need. We do not sell your personal data to third parties or marketing agencies.

3. Data Visibility

When you post a blood request, your contact information may be visible to registered donors. If you register as a donor, your contact details are kept secure and are only shared when you explicitly agree to fulfill a request.

4. Security

We take security seriously and use industry-standard encryption for passwords and sensitive data to protect your information.
