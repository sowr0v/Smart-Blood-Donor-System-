📋 System Data Retention & Privacy Scrubbing Policy

When you submit an account deletion request, our automated Data Privacy Scrubbing routine decouples and purges personal identifiers while maintaining aggregate public health records for medical transparency:

🗑️ Personal Data Purged & Scrubbed

  • Full Legal Name: Replaced with a cryptographic anonymous hash ([Scrubbed Donor - ID: UUID]).
  • Contact Phone & Email: Decoupled and purged from all public directories and notification tables.
  • Physical Location: Exact address, district, area, and GPS coordinates permanently erased.
  • Identity Documents: KYC verification files, NID numbers, and uploaded credentials deleted.
  • Active Sessions: Immediate invalidation of all authentication tokens, cookies, and open devices.

🛡️ Retained Operational Records

  • Donation Volumes: Completed blood unit metrics (e.g. 450 mL units donated) preserved for health facility inventory records.
  • Blood Type Statistics: Non-identifying ABO/Rh statistical counts preserved for public health reporting.
  • Donation Timestamps: Historical completion dates retained without any link to your identity.
  • Regulatory Auditing: Immutable record that user account ID was scrubbed in compliance with safety policies.
